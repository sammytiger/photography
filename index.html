My story

How I found the camera

Keep scrolling ↓

i.

I was burned out in a corporate job. Most days ended at the same desk they started at.

ii.

So I picked up a camera.

iii.

I pointed it at Sammy instead of a screen.

iv.

Then at my black kitten, mid-prowl.

v.

Then the mountains. Then the creek with friends, shoes off, nobody posing.

vi.

Now I photograph other people's lives the way I learned to see mine: slowly, in good light.

How it works

Simple on your end.

01

Tell me what's going on

Who's coming, roughly when, and anything I should know: a new baby, a dog who hates leashes, a business launch.

02

We pick a place and a time

I'll suggest a spot that fits your people and the light, plus a short what-to-wear note so nobody's stressing the night before.

03

You get a private gallery

Edited photos in an online gallery you can share and download in full resolution. Prints if you want them on the wall.

About

Hi, I'm Christine.

I've spent my career watching how people actually live. What they reach for, what they ignore, what makes them relax. That turns out to be most of what a good photographer does, too.

I photograph families on walks they'd take anyway, founders in the rooms where their work happens, and small gatherings where people are glad to be together.

I live in Boulder with Sammy, a red cattle dog with strong opinions about every session involving another dog, and a small black kitten with strong opinions about everything else.
