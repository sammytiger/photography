Where is the session?
Somewhere beautiful and close to town. I'll confirm the exact location when I reply, based on the light, the weather, and where you're staying.

    
What if it snows?
Snow makes the best holiday portraits. If it's truly unsafe, we move to a covered spot or shift your time.

    
When do we get our photos?
Your private gallery arrives within a week. Concierge families get their edits first.

    
When do the cards go out?
Proofs come by email for one quick approval. Cards print right after, in time to arrive well before Christmas.

    
Can grandparents or the dog join?
Yes, and please. Tell me who's coming so I can plan the time and the setup.
